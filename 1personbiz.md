### Step 1 : Founders Triangle
1. Domain
2. Depth
3. Distribution

When you have a great idea apply the "Founder's Triangle"

### Step 2: The Machinery
> Daily details and everyday execution

5 core functions: DREAM functions
1. Demand: qualified pipeline of leads. No pipeline => No revenue. How do you find customers? How do customers find you? This is your lead gen engine
2. Revenue: Pricing, Packaging, Renewal Mechanics, Profit Margin. Todo esto tiene que estar muy claro
3. Engine: core product/service/platform. How it fits the market needs. How to build what your customers will buy
4. Admin: back office. Finance, accounting, legal, billing, contracts. Things that keep the business running smoothly
5. Marketing: how do you build reputation and brand through content, communities, connection, case studies?

This is the business foundation, your dream machine running 24/7.
You can build it with AI. Focus in one piece. One area.

### Step 3: The Moast
> Protección del revenue

El problema real del mundo de IA no es comenzar un negocio, es mantenerse en el negocio.
Ejemplo: blockbuster de 6B a bancarrota, NEtflix de 50M a 500B
#### Moat 1 - Counter Positioning
si vendes un producto de un modo que ataca el core business model of your competition, you can take the their market share. They'll see what you're doing, but they win't be able to respond to you without cannibalizing their own business.
MAs ejemplos: SouthWest Airline, Airbnb, Dollar Shave Club

#### Moat 2 - High Switching Cost
> Sticky habits

Leaving you is very painful. google is a habit vs bing. iPhone users don't switch to Android.
Now the new habit is chatGPT, google gemini is there, but people are used to chatGPT.
How do you make your product a habit?

#### Moat 3 - Proprietary Data & Learning Loops
google, Amazon, Uber, ChatGPT => Gold mine of consumer data. They use that data to refine their products all the time. That generate more data for these companies and is a self-reinforcing loop

Is there any propieraty data that you have? how do you get it? Is there a prtnership you can strike?
Ejemplo: cursor tiene un feedback loop que analiza keystrokes de sus usuarios y en base a eso genera un feature por día

### Step 4 - The Mindset
> Acepta el miedo, la duda y hacelo igual.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> La pregunta mas importante: cuando este en mi lecho de muerte, cuál será mi mayor arrepentimiento?

AI is gonna change every industry and it;s changing almost every career.
Estamos en este momento del tiempo donde IA casi se vuelve más inteligente qeu cualquier humano.
In this new world, What will you bring to the table? Your taste, purpose, relationships, judgement, critical thinking
Cosas que la IA no puede replicar o automatizar.

> [!DANGER]
> When you have self doubts, that is the mindset shift you have to make. You don't need permission from others. The voice in your head will always force you to pick regret over risk. You need to allow yourself to succeed

The risks you take and fail, have much less impact than the risks you fail to take
