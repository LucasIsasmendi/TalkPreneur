- DElegation Priority Score = (Frecuency + Annoyance + Impact + Simplicity) / 4
- Decide
  - Delete: if it's no-impact busy work
  - Automate: is there a tool to do it
  - Delegate: someone can do it 80% like you. Document and delegate

For every task, who is the current owner, who should be the `ideal owner`.
- if you didn't have to do everything, what you'll choose to do in your business
- ideal owner: who you should hire

Before you delegate, document
- do you have checklist?
- make templates for anything repeatable
